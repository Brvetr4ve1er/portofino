About

◆ THE BUILDER

Mohamed Yasser Hamisse — a designer & developer out of Bordj Bou Arréridj, Algeria (currently between BBA and Tunis). Six years across freelance and studio work, currently designing for Heetch international while building products for local businesses back home.

The through-line is no middlemen: whoever scopes a project ships it. That means one pair of hands taking a travel agency, a clinic, or a steel manufacturer from brand and identity all the way to a deployed, maintained platform — in French, English or Arabic, RTL included.

The other half is a heavy data addiction: document-intelligence pipelines, live probability scrapers, product-intelligence tooling, and a personal "HUD" for staying on task. The web work pays the bills; the data work is where the obsession lives.

Studio home: Numidea.tech — "rooted in Numidia, built on ideas."
